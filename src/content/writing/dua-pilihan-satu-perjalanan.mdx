When applications opened for the IME FTUI 2026 board, one question kept coming back to me: *which division should I join?* Kominfo? Academics and Professional Development, or AKPRO? Or something else entirely?

At first, I wanted to join AKPRO. Back when I lived in a dorm, I served as the education minister in our dorm organization and taught informatics olympiad material. I enjoyed explaining things until they made sense to someone else, so AKPRO felt like a natural first choice.

But along the way, I started wondering: *what if I try something new?*

I also enjoy design: making things, playing with visual ideas, and having room to be creative. That was what drew me to Kominfo. I was still interested in teaching; I was simply curious about a different experience too.

## Two choices, and a little doubt

During recruitment, we could list two divisions in order of preference. In the end, I put Kominfo first and AKPRO second.

To be honest, there was another reason for that order: **I doubted myself.**

A lot of people were applying to AKPRO. To me, they all seemed talented and smart. The competition felt tough, and I started wondering whether I was good enough. My curiosity about Kominfo was real, but that uncertainty also played a part in my decision.

## Plot twist: I got to do both

Long story short, Kominfo board members could also support another IME division through the *sahabat bidang* program. Since I had been interested in AKPRO from the start, I eventually had the chance to support that division as well.

I could keep creating in Kominfo while staying involved with the division I had originally wanted to join. I got both of them. A little win-win solution, right there.

Being part of Kominfo IME FTUI 2026 turned out a little differently from what I had imagined, and that was part of what made it enjoyable.
